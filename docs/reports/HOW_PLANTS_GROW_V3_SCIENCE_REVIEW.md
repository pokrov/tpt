## Open human checks

- Verify the generated plant and five stage illustrations against actual bean morphology, especially flowering, pod placement, root branching and the page 3 anatomy pointers.
- Review every answer and question for grade-level clarity, including the light-energy classification and the arrow-drawing instruction on page 9.
- Confirm the seed observation protocol is suitable for the intended classroom and seed variety. The book asks students to record actual observations and accepts no germination as a valid result.
