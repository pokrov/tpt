## Gaps and decisions

1. **Science:** The current review compares questions with their authored passage and has a blind answer solver, but no independently sourced science claim ledger or human science approval. Passage support alone can certify a false premise as internally consistent. For the plant book, the supplied audit identifies a critical page 8/9 error and major issues on pages 2–7 and 10–11. Source-backed human checking remains necessary.
2. **Product format:** The active reading contract is five passage/QCM pairs, cover and one final key. The spec's observation protocol, log, diagrams, short responses, teacher support, US Letter, and 16–24 useful pages are a new product profile. Simply raising page counts or changing the shared reading template would regress existing books and both grades. Build a scoped V2 plant/reading-and-observation profile first; decide separately whether to generalize it.
3. **Question dependencies:** Existing evidence and key binding are substantial, but no persistent question inventory or versioned passage-to-question invalidation spans an editor's later revisions. Use stable IDs and hashes for final canonical passage, diagrams, question, answer, and evidence. A changed source blocks approval until its dependents are independently checked again.
4. **Quality state:** `content_checks.status='passed'` describes generation checks. It is not a human publication decision. No durable draft, blocker, human approval, rejection, or provenance status exists. A score must be computed from review evidence and never clear a critical blocker.
5. **Provenance:** The saved `.product.json` intentionally omits solver/provider payloads and answers; it is listing metadata, not a rights ledger. Images are temporary. The old logo, AI artwork, fonts and passage rights are unresolved; the new supplied logo also needs ownership/usage confirmation. A rights record may say `Unverified`; it must not invent a license.
6. **Print design:** The current renderer's `@page size:A4`, 186 mm article width, 265 mm cover, 88 mm logo crop and key fit logic are coupled. The new square logo needs cover-specific adjustment. US Letter and low-ink output require separate measured layouts; converting the PDF to grayscale would not meet the spec. Preserve readable student type and writing space.
7. **Schedule/cost:** Saved `local_schedule` overrides `.env.local`. The present eight-book daily schedule conflicts with the preferred manual workflow and can consume provider quota while the app runs. The first approved operational action should be to disable that saved schedule through the existing interface/API while idle; do not edit `.env.local` or clear `local_daily` history. A manual test generation must remain one book and should occur only with the user's approval for provider quota use.
8. **User-instruction scope:** Standing project instructions call for 12-page reading/QCM, no teacher guide, no drawing, and one final key. The V2 brief explicitly requests a teacher quick start, observation sketch area, mixed assessment and potentially two key pages. Implement these only in the new V2 profile; preserve existing default products and current grade gates. Keep teacher content separate from student pages and do not label unsupported AI illustrations as exact scientific diagrams.

## Data, provider cost, regressions, and verification

- **Schema/storage:** Introduce a versioned canonical V2 workbook schema and per-book quality/provenance sidecars with atomic writes and content/PDF hashes. Store only small index/status pointers in existing `state.json` if needed. No database migration is proposed. Older PDFs and `.product.json` remain readable; absent sidecars mean `Unverified`, never approved.
- **Provider use:** Current reading generation already uses a planning completion, five author completions, five review completions, blind answer verification and image calls, with additional bounded repair calls possible. V2 adds at least one targeted science review completion per relevant passage or book plus any scoped corrections; exact token and Cloudflare costs cannot be estimated reliably from this checkout because configured model, actual usage, quota plan and provider rates are not recorded here. Before enabling live V2 generation, expose per-book call/token counts where supplied and set a hard maximum for extra review calls; provide an estimated dollar cost from the user's verified provider rates. No new paid service or automatic quota-consuming run is proposed.
- **Main regressions:** changing A4 geometry could clip both grades; enlarging a key could overflow; new mixed tasks could leak answers to student pages; science edits could leave stale key prose; durable sidecars could become stale when PDFs change; a scheduler change could erase attempt metadata; accidental global branding or product-profile changes could affect old formats. Isolate V2 and validate hashes and grade gates.
- **After approval only:** Add meaningful fixtures for false plant taxonomy, photosynthesis and transport claims, question/key dependency invalidation, review status transitions, provenance blockers, US Letter/low-ink rendering, and old-book compatibility. Run focused existing tests plus the appropriate broader suite; use mocked provider calls first. Render every page of both V2 PDFs and inspect visually, extract text/key order, inspect output/sidecar hashes, and confirm old PDF hash unchanged. Any live Gemini/Cloudflare draft generation needs explicit quota authorization. Check the active worker before any Docker rebuild; then verify health, interface, download and persistent-file hashes on the rebuilt local image.
