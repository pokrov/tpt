# How Plants Grow V3 design system — prototype

Direction: **illustrated botanical cover, clean classroom interior**. This is a two-page design proposal. It does not change the workbook renderer or imply scientific/commercial approval.

| Role | HEX | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | `#183F35` | Title, folio, primary text accents |
| Moss | `#46745A` | Secondary headings, botanical accents |
| Pale sage | `#DDE9DF` | Small classroom information bands |
| Warm cream | `#F7F3E8` | Cover background only |
| Paper | `#FFFDF8` | Reading page background |
| Ochre | `#B8924A` | Thin cover rules |
| Ink | `#1D302A` | Reading text |

US Letter, 612 × 792 pt. Cover margins 52 pt; interior 48–51 pt. Title uses Times Bold at 44 pt on the cover and 27 pt on the reading page. Supporting text uses Helvetica around 9–15 pt; the passage is 11.5 pt with 17 pt leading. Times and Helvetica are standard PDF fonts in ReportLab; actual rendering/redistribution terms still require a commercial review. No external font files are bundled in the prototype.

Use one dominant botanical illustration per page. The cover art carries the story from dry bean to seedling and needs no decorative arrows. On the reading page, a separate seed cutaway and germinating bean complement the unchanged passage. All labels are native PDF text, positioned outside the artwork. Treat label pointers as editorial annotations requiring expert review; generated art is never the sole source of an answer.

The reading page uses a restrained band for name/date and a compact three-column vocabulary strip. A single horizontal rule separates the passage from the study illustration. The footer carries brand, draft status and page number. Keep the passage clear of ornament; leave generous white space around the botanical study. For the ink variant, backgrounds and filled bands are removed and the same illustration is converted to a light grayscale derivative without changing its anatomy. A full workbook adaptation should apply this system consistently after the user approves these two pages.

Thumbnail check: “How Plants Grow” and “Grades 3–4” should remain readable at small preview size; the central four-stage bean story remains identifiable. Do not add seller badges, unsupported standards claims, or unverified review statistics.
