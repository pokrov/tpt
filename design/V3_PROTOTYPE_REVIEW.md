# V3 two-page visual review

3 November 2026. Prototype only; no workbook-library change, Docker rebuild, provider generation or TPT publication.

## Inspection

Both US Letter pages in `design/prototypes/v3-two-page-preview.pdf` were rendered to 1600-pixel-tall PNGs and inspected at full resolution. The cover was also checked at a 260-pixel-wide listing thumbnail. The separate ink reading PDF was rendered and inspected. PDF extraction confirmed two 612 × 792 pt pages, the exact reading title/objective, both V2.1 passage paragraphs, all five required diagram labels, and all three vocabulary terms. The ink PDF contains one page.

**Cover:** The botanical sequence is the visual centerpiece, and the title remains readable at thumbnail size. The grade badge is visible but its small text and the supporting tagline will be hard to read in a very small marketplace thumbnail. It is a stronger direction than V2.1's line-drawn cover, but teacher response and TPT conversion are untested.

**Reading page:** The passage has a clear hierarchy, comfortable measure and white space. The illustration and vocabulary strip support the text while occupying less ink than the cover. The final label leaders no longer visibly cross text or overlap one another. The ink version removes filled bands and uses a light grayscale derivative of the same botanical artwork. Physical print quality and ink consumption have not been measured.

**Scientific limitation:** The reading illustration was generated, so its internal anatomy and leader-line targets remain **unverified**. The seed coat, embryo, stored food/cotyledons, emerging root and shoot must be checked by a qualified educator or botanist. The artwork must not be treated as a verified scientific diagram or as an answer source before that review. Commercial-use rights for generated assets, brand and fonts are also Unverified.

## Applying an approved direction

If the user approves the look, carry its typography, color and spacing into a dedicated V3 workbook template while retaining the existing canonical questions, science checks, answer keys, observation space, ink option and human approval gates. Make scientific diagrams from expert-reviewed art/labels before distributing student pages. Do not replace V2/V2.1 or publish anything during that adaptation.
