## Direction chosen

The verified listings emphasize readable science passages, diagrams/activities tied to the text, answer keys, vocabulary and print options. That does **not** establish a universal cover style or prove that a particular aesthetic sells. My design interpretation is a botanical editorial cover with one unmistakable bean-growth illustration, a large title and a concise grade cue. The reading page uses the same green/cream identity with much less ink, generous text measure, labeled artwork and a compact vocabulary strip. The cover and page are original and intentionally do not imitate any specific seller.
